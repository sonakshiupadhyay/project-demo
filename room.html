Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know regarding check-in times, luxury amenities, and booking policies.

What are the check-in and check-out times?
Standard check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is until 12:00 PM (Noon). Early check-in and late check-out options up to 4:00 PM are available upon request for Executive & Presidential Suite guests.
Is airport transfer included with room bookings?
Complimentary luxury limousine transfers (Rolls-Royce or Mercedes-Maybach) are included with all Presidential Suites, Royal Penthouses, and Private Pool Villas. Transfer service can also be added to any room tier during checkout.
What is the cancellation and refund policy?
We offer 100% Free Cancellation up to 24 hours prior to check-in for standard flex rates. Special non-refundable discounted packages are highlighted clearly during reservation.
Are breakfast and dining options included?
All rooms booked via our official portal include complimentary gourmet breakfast buffet at our Horizon Restaurant or private in-room suite dining service.
